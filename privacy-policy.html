Imperial CFS Cookie and Privacy Policy

Imperial CFS understands the importance of privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Cookie and Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how we use cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website. By accessing or using our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this Policy.

We treat your personal information with the utmost respect which is why we never sell your information to third party marketers. The information we collect is only to create a smooth experience across our website and to provide information regarding shipments. From time to time we may send out emails to notify you of any changes in our policies or practices but you may opt out any time.

What Are Cookies?


Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to provide information to website owners, and to improve your overall website experience.

What are the benefits of cookies?


Cookies can store information such as login information or shipment information to create a streamless and convenient experience across our website.

Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following types of cookies on our website:

  • Session Cookies:
    These cookies maintain information about the user's login status across our website and are one of the most common tyles of cookies. When users log in, cookies can store session IDs or tokens to keep you logged in across pages. While you may use our site to track shipments without logging in, it will keep track of which shipment you are searching from page to page. Once you exit, your browser will delete all session cookies.
  • Essential Cookies:
    These cookies are necessary for the functioning of our website. They enable you to navigate the website and use its features. Without these cookies, some services on our website may not be available.
  • Analytical/Performance Cookies:
    These cookies allow us to analyze how visitors use our website. They help us improve the website's functionality and user experience. For example, we may use Google Analytics, a widely used analytic plug-in to track website traffic and user behavior. These are non-identifiable habits that give us insight on how to better maintain a smooth user experience.
  • Payment Processing Related Cookies:
    Our site offers e-payment options and the information you provide will be stored across pages and to third party credit card/e-check processors. Imperial does not store any of the information provided and third party processors follow their own privacy policies. We may retain your email address for sole purposes of sending receipts and confirmation of payments or guarantees.
  • Permanent cookies:
    remain in operation even after you close the web browser. For example, these cookies can be used to remember your login details and passwords so you do not need to re-enter them every time you use our site.
  • Your Cookie Choices
    You can control and manage cookies in your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to control and manage cookies through their settings. You can configure your browser to accept, reject, or delete cookies. Please note that disabling cookies may affect your experience on our website, and some features may not work as intended.

Updates to This Policy


We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The website will be updated each time the policy is updated. If you have any questions or concerns about this Cookie Policy or our use of cookies, please contact us at info@imperialcfs.net
